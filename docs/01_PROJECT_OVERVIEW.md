# DentaBook - Project Overview & Scope

## 1. Executive Summary

**DentaBook** is a modern web-based dental appointment and clinic management platform. It addresses common pain points in dental healthcare by providing transparency in pricing, simplifying clinic and dentist discovery, streamlining the booking process for patients, and empowering dental clinics with dedicated tools to manage their schedules, services, and appointments.

---

## 2. Problem Statement & Core Goals

### The Problem
- **Pricing Ambiguity**: Patients often hesitate to seek dental care due to unpredictable or undisclosed treatment costs.
- **Booking Friction**: Scheduling appointments typically requires phone calls during office hours, leading to scheduling conflicts and missed appointments.
- **Administrative Burden**: Clinics struggle with manual schedule tracking, appointment confirmations, and patient inquiries.

### Project Goals
1. **Cost Transparency**: Enable patients to browse dental treatments, review estimated prices, and calculate aggregate costs before booking.
2. **Seamless Booking**: Provide a 24/7 online scheduling experience with real-time slot selection and down-payment processing.
3. **Operational Efficiency**: Equip clinics with a clean management dashboard to configure dentist credentials, operating hours, service catalogs, and appointment states.

---

## 3. User Roles & Personas

| Role | Description | Core Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Patient / Customer** | Individuals seeking dental treatments | Browse clinics/dentists, calculate estimated fees, book time slots, pay down payments, track/reschedule visits, leave reviews. |
| **Clinic Staff / Dentist** | Verified dental clinics and practitioners | Manage clinic profiles, dentist credentials, service catalogs, availability calendars, review incoming requests, track payment statuses. |
| **System Administrator** | Platform overseer | Review and approve new clinic registrations, maintain platform integrity and policies. |

---

## 4. Key Platform Modules

1. **Discovery & Clinic Profiles**: Showcase clinic background, physical location, contact info, operating hours, and dentist accreditations.
2. **Interactive Cost Calculator**: Dynamic treatment estimator calculating upfront and aggregate estimated costs.
3. **Booking & Reservation Engine**: Interactive calendar with dentist-specific availability slots and automated conflict prevention.
4. **Clinic Admin Dashboard**: Protected workspace for clinic administrators and practitioners to review appointments, adjust prices, and manage schedules.
5. **Appointment Tracking & Notifications**: Status updates for accepted, pending, rescheduled, or completed visits.
